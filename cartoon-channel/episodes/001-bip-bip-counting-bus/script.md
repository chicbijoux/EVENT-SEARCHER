# Episode 001: The Bip-Bip Counting Bus

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lesson** | Counting 1-2-3, plus animal sounds |
| **Runtime** | ~33 s (3 s hook + 6 scenes × 5 s) |
| **Cast** | Leo, Bibo, Tofu, plus one-off passengers: 1 duckling, 2 frogs, 3 piglets |
| **Settings** | Leo's backyard → Lollipop Hills → backyard |
| **Sing-along line** | *"Bip-bip, bip-bip, who's getting on?"* (sung 3 times) |

## Story in one line

Leo honks his toy bus Bibo three times, Bibo grows into a real bus, and they drive through
Lollipop Hills counting the animal friends who hop aboard: one duck, two frogs, three piglets.
Then Bibo shrinks back into Leo's hands, right where the Short began.

**If the music model can't sing the lyrics cleanly:** make an instrumental with the same prompt (drop the vocal line), then

works, and the kids' chorus is optional.

## Why the song is a separate track

Every clip is generated on its own. If each clip made its own music, the tune would jump at
every cut. So:

- **Clips:** sound effects only. Every prompt ends with *"no music, no speech"*.
- **Song:** one continuous track for the whole Short.
- **CapCut:** song at 100%, clip audio at ~60%, so the quacks and horn sit inside the song.

## Edit notes (CapCut)

1. New project → **9:16**. Drop in clips 0–6 in order.
2. Add the song. Line up each lyric with its scene, and trim or speed clips slightly (0.9–1.1×) to hit the beats.
3. **Trim any slow first second** of every clip, especially the hook. It must be moving at frame 1.
4. Add one big bouncy number ("1", "2", "3") popping in the top third at each stop. This reinforces the lesson and survives any AI number glitches on the bus-stop signs.
5. Check the loop: the last frame (toy Bibo filling the frame) should flow straight back into the hook.
6. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps.
