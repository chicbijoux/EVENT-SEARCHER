18:40Arrival

The door slides open at twenty to seven.

Come a few minutes early. Coats go on the hooks by the door, and each guest is shown to the seat they chose when they booked.

At seven the curtain drops behind the last guest and the room goes quiet. Nobody else comes in tonight.

One piece, in four movements.

    21:05The last word

    Every piece, the same two hands.

    There is no kitchen behind the wall. The chef works alone at the counter, so each piece you eat is made for you, a few seconds before you eat it.

    At the end of the evening the chef asks which piece you would like again. That one is on the house.
