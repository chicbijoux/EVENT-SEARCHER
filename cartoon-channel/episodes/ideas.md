# Episode ideas backlog

Nine more ideas to round out the first week of 10. Each one follows the ritual in
`channel-bible.md`: hook → "Bip, bip, BIG!" → three stops → the ride → "Bip, bip, small!".

| # | Working title | Lesson | The three stops | Sing-along line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 002 | Bibo's Rainbow Windows | Colors | Bibo's windows turn red, then yellow, then blue at each stop | "Bip-bip, what color now?" |
| 003 | Stop and Go! | Traffic lights | Red light: stop · yellow: slow · green: go! | "Red says stop, green says go!" |
| 004 | Shape Stops | Shapes | Circle stop, square stop, triangle stop | "Bip-bip, what shape is that?" |
| 005 | Bibo Gets a Bath | Washing / habits | Muddy puddle → bubble car wash → shiny towel dry | "Scrub, scrub, splish-splash!" |
| 006 | Big and Small | Opposites | Big elephant / small mouse · tall giraffe / short turtle · loud lion / quiet bunny | "Bip, bip, BIG… bip, bip, small!" |
| 007 | Who Says Moo? | Farm animal sounds | Cow, sheep, rooster | "Bip-bip, who says…?" |
| 008 | Rainy Day Ride | Weather | Drizzle → puddles → rainbow | "Swish, swish, go the wipers!" |
| 009 | Tofu Shares Her Seat | Sharing | A kitten, a hedgehog and a bunny need a seat | "There's room for one more!" |
| 010 | Goodnight, Bibo | Bedtime (calm) | Moon stop, stars stop, sleepy owl stop | "Headlights low, time to go… to sleep" |
